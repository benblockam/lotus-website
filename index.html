The company

Lotus EdTech Limited is a financial technology and digital infrastructure firm that bridges the gap between sophisticated institutional strategies and accessible, data-driven consumer platforms. Serving as a consolidated hub for fintech initiatives, the firm builds transparent, technology-enabled tools that empower better financial decision-making.

Our team holds advanced degrees from UC Berkeley, Stanford and HKU.

UC Berkeley Stanford HKU
Core focus areas
01

Retirement & Wealth Tech

The firm is the driving force behind MPFGo, a specialized digital portal that brings institutional-grade data analytics to Hong Kong's Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF) system. The platform demystifies retirement planning through intuitive investment tracking, historical performance metrics, and clear overviews of market cycles.

02

Digital Assets & Quantitative Strategies

Lotus EdTech builds infrastructure and data analytics for digital assets, applying the same institutional-grade rigour that underpins its retirement tools. The firm develops systematic frameworks for measuring on-chain activity, market structure, and risk — turning noisy crypto markets into clear, quantitative signals. The objective is consistent across every asset class: making complexity measurable, and decisions better informed.

03

Educational Technology

By transforming complex financial data into actionable, easily digestible insights, the firm embodies its “EdTech” mandate — educating everyday investors and market participants through clear technology and analytics.
